Towson International Limited — Effective 2 June 2026

OUTSMART respects your right to control your data. This page explains exactly how to request deletion of any data we hold about you, what we will delete, and how long it takes.

1. How to Request Deletion

Email us from any address using the following format. We do not need you to verify your identity, because deletion requests cause no harm if processed against the wrong account.

  1. Send an email to support@playoutsmart.com
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request
  3. In the body, include your leaderboard display name (e.g. KIBIRU) and, if known, your country code (e.g. KE). If you do not remember your display name, tell us the approximate date you joined the leaderboard.

Email Us Now

2. What We Will Delete

On confirmation of your request, we will permanently delete:

  • Your leaderboard entry (display name, points, wins, total games played, subscription status)
  • Your country code, if associated with your entry
  • Your anonymous Firebase Authentication identifier
  • Any moderation reports you submitted
  • Any moderation reports submitted against your display name

3. What We Cannot Delete

Some data is outside our control or required by law to retain:

  • Purchase records: managed by Google Play or Apple, not by us. Use your store account settings or contact those platforms to request purchase data deletion.
  • Aggregated analytics: Google AdMob's ad-serving logs and aggregate metrics are governed by Google's policies. See Google's partner site policy .
  • Tax and accounting records: subscription transaction history that we are required to retain for legal and tax purposes is kept according to applicable law.

4. How Long Deletion Takes

We will confirm your request and complete deletion within 30 days of receipt. Most requests are processed within 7 business days. Once deletion is complete, we will send you a confirmation email.

5. Reinstalling the App

Uninstalling OUTSMART clears all on-device data immediately. If you want to also delete data we hold on our servers (leaderboard entry, moderation records), follow the steps above. Reinstalling the app after deletion will treat you as a brand new user.

If you have questions about this process, or need help recovering your display name to make a deletion request, contact:
