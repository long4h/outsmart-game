Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 April 2026

OUTSMART is designed with a privacy-first approach — no accounts, no login, and minimal data collection. OUTSMART is a product of Towson International Limited.

1. Information We Collect

OUTSMART is designed to be played without creating an account or providing personal information.

If you choose to join the leaderboard, you provide a display name (chosen by you). This name, along with your game stats (points, wins, total games played), your subscription status, and your country code are stored on our servers to power the leaderboard.

Your country is detected automatically on first launch using your IP address via a third-party service (ipapi.co). Only the two-letter country code (e.g. "KE") is stored — your full IP address is not retained by us.

All other gameplay data (settings, streak, daily challenge progress, hint usage, ad history) is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers.

2. Advertising

OUTSMART shows ads powered by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data to serve personalised or non-personalised ads, including your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS), device information, and coarse location.

On iOS, we do not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, so ads are served without cross-app tracking.

To learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services, visit: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • operate and display the leaderboard;
  • detect your country for country-based leaderboard filtering;
  • serve ads that support the free version of the app;
  • respond to support requests if you contact us directly.

We do not use your information for any other purpose.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

Leaderboard data (display name, points, wins, country, subscription status) is visible to other players in the app.

We share data with Google (AdMob and Firebase/Firestore) to operate advertising and leaderboard services. Google's privacy policy applies to their handling of this data.

5. Data Retention

Leaderboard entries remain until you delete your entry or request removal by contacting us. Device-only data is cleared when you uninstall the app.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. Leaderboard data is stored in Google Firestore with standard security controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

OUTSMART is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will remove it promptly.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • You may request deletion of your leaderboard entry at any time by contacting us.
  • You can reset all local game data by uninstalling the app.
  • You may opt out of personalised ads through your device's advertising settings (Android: Google Settings → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking).

9. Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Firebase / Firestore — leaderboard storage
  • ipapi.co — country detection
  • Capacitor Local Notifications — local push notifications (no data sent externally)

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these services. Please review their respective privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this policy will be updated. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
